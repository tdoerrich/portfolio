I joined **Gunzilla Games** early in the development of *[OFF THE GRID](https://playoffthegrid.com/)* as a Senior Level Designer. When I started, the studio had two junior level designers, but not much else in place for level design.

I ended up leading the team, setting goals, and putting rules in place for how we built levels, and was later promoted to Lead Level Designer.

### My Role

**Position:** Senior Level Designer, later Lead Level Designer    
**Scope:** Level prototyping, team leadership, and setting up the level design pipeline    
**Duration:** April 2021 – April 2022    

**Senior Level Designer:** April 2021 – October 2021    
**Lead Level Designer:** November 2021 – April 2022    

My responsibilities included:

* Prototyping early level concepts ahead of full production
* Setting goals for the level design team
* Creating the rules and guidelines for building levels
* Working with the art lead on a pipeline for our two teams
* Helping build out the level design team
* Mentoring the junior and regular level designers

### Working with Art

A big part of setting things up was working with the art lead on a pipeline we both agreed on, so our teams had a clear way of working together.
