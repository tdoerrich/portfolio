During my time at **YAGER**, I worked as a Lead Level Designer across several projects, from an early-stage mid-core mobile shooter to *Dune: Awakening* and *Exoborne*.

My role changed depending on the project, but a large part of my work was leading level designers, helping establish how we approached level design, and working closely with our external partners.

## Dune: Awakening

![Header](/assets/img/dune-header.jpg)

During YAGER’s co-development with Funcom on *Dune: Awakening*, I led the level design work on the **modular crashed ship system**.

The work involved both leading the designers working on the system and figuring out how the modular pieces could be used from a level design perspective. I also worked directly with Funcom's directors to review the designs and keep our work aligned with the wider project.

### My Role

**Position:** Lead Level Designer
**Scope:** Team leadership, design direction, and hands-on design support
**Duration:** 2022–2024

My responsibilities included:

* Guiding the level design team through planning, blockout, iteration, and polish
* Mentoring designers and helping them work through design problems
* Reviewing designs with Funcom and coordinating feedback between the two studios
* Establishing workflows and guidelines for building the modular crashed ships
* Reviewing the team's work and helping keep the direction consistent

### Process

**Concept & Blockout**

I oversaw the initial blockouts and helped establish guidelines for scale, usability, and how the modular pieces would work with the game's other systems.

**Production**

As production progressed, I reviewed iterations with the team, ran playthroughs, and helped identify and solve design issues. A significant part of the role was also communicating with Funcom and making sure feedback was understood and incorporated by the team.

**Polish & Modular Implementation**

I helped refine the modular crashed ship layouts and the way the pieces were used, with a focus on making the system practical for the level designers using it.

### Challenges

**Building a reusable modular system**

The crashed ships needed to work across different locations without every level becoming a one-off solution.

I worked with the art team to establish guidelines and documentation for using the modular pieces, giving the level designers a consistent foundation to work from.

## Exoborne

![Header](/assets/img/exoborne-header.jpg)

For *Exoborne*, I led the YAGER level design team during their work on **Agnesville**, an extraction-based shooter developed by Sharkmob.

My role was less about building the level myself and more about directing the work of the level design team. I worked with a Senior Level Designer who handled the day-to-day implementation while I focused on the overall direction, reviews, and coordination with Sharkmob.

### My Role

**Position:** Lead Level Designer
**Scope:** Level direction, team leadership, and design reviews
**Duration:** 2024–2025

As Lead, I was responsible for:

* Working with Sharkmob's directors and design leads
* Making larger design decisions around flow, pacing, and spatial layout
* Mentoring and delegating to the level designers on the team
* Reviewing blockouts and later iterations
* Playtesting and giving feedback throughout production
* Getting the level ready for release

I gave the Senior Level Designer ownership of the day-to-day implementation while staying involved in the larger design decisions and regular reviews.

### Level Focus

The main areas I focused on were:

* Player flow and pacing
* Spatial design
* Integration of partner-provided assets and systems

### Process

**Concept**

I worked with Sharkmob's World Director to understand the high-level goals for the level and passed this on to my team as we built the location.

**Production**

Once the direction was established, I reviewed the team's iterations and helped solve design problems as they came up. I also coordinated feedback between YAGER and Sharkmob to keep the level aligned with the project's goals and technical constraints.

**Polish & Release**

Towards release, I focused on playtesting, design reviews, and getting the level ready for release.

### Working as a Lead

One of the more important parts of my role on *Exoborne* was giving other designers real ownership of their work.

Rather than handling the daily implementation myself, I gave the Senior Level Designer responsibility for the execution of the level. I stayed involved through regular reviews and playtests, providing direction where needed while allowing them to make the day-to-day design decisions.

## Other work at YAGER
