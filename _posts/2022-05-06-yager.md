## Dune: Awakening

![Header](/assets/img/dune-header.jpg)

During YAGER’s co-development with Funcom on *Dune: Awakening*, I led the level design efforts for the modular crashed ship systems. My role focused on **team leadership, design strategy, and coordination with the external partner studio**.

### My Role
**Position:** Lead Level Designer  
**Scope:** Mentorship, strategic oversight, and design execution support  
**Duration:** [2022-2024]  

Responsibilities included:  
- Guiding YAGER’s level design team through planning and execution  
- Mentoring designers on iteration, blockout, and polish processes  
- Coordinating design reviews and feedback with Funcom directors  
- Establishing workflows for modular level used for the crashed ships  
- Ensuring quality standards were met across all level design work

### Process
**Concept & Blockout:**  
- Oversaw early design blockouts for modular systems  
- Defined guidelines for scale, usability, and integration with gameplay systems  

**Production:**  
- Provided strategic oversight and direction to the level design team  
- Facilitated iterative testing, playthroughs, and integration checks  
- Coordinated with Funcom for alignment and review  

**Polish & Modular Implementation:**  
- Assisted in refining modular crashed ships for consistent quality and usability  

### Challenges & Solutions
**Challenge:** Collaborating with an external studio while maintaining quality and deadlines  
**Solution:** Established clear communication protocols, structured feedback sessions, and milestone-driven reviews  

**Challenge:** Ensuring modular assets could be reused efficiently across different areas  
**Solution:** Developed guidelines and documentation together with the art team to support consistent use by the team  

## Key Achievements
- Successfully led YAGER’s level design contributions on a high-profile co-dev project  
- Mentored designers to take ownership of individual elements  
- Delivered modular crashed ship systems on time for integration  
- Implemented workflows that improved team efficiency and external collaboration  


## Exoborne

![Header](/assets/img/exoborne-header.jpg)


Agnesville is one of the levels in Exoborne an extraction-based shooter, built by Sharkmob. I led the level design from initial concept to release-ready state, overseeing both strategic decisions and team execution.

### My Role
**Position:** Lead Level Designer  
**Scope:** Full level oversight (concept → final)  
**Duration:** [2024-2025]  

As Lead, I was responsible for:  
- Overall level direction and quality  
- Communication with Sharkmob directors and design leads
- Strategic design decisions  
- Team mentorship and delegation (empowered Senior LD to own execution)
- Design reviews, playtesting, and iteration guidance
- Final sign-off and release readiness validation

I empowered a Senior Level Designer to handle daily design execution, while I provided strategic oversight and ensured the team had clear ownership and accountability.

### Level Focus  
- Player flow, pacing, and spatial design  
- Integration of partner-provided assets and systems

### Process
**Concept Phase:**  
- Defined high-level gameplay goals and flow  
- Created early blockouts for testing pacing and navigation  
- Aligned design with Sharkmob expectations  

**Production:**  
- Supported execution by reviewing iterations and providing guidance  
- Ensured design decisions matched project goals and technical constraints  
- Coordinated cross-studio feedback and approvals  

**Polish & Release:**  
- Conducted final quality checks  
- Ensured level was fully playable and met production standards  
- Mentored team members through final iteration challenges  

### Challenges & Solutions
**Challenge:** Empowering a Senior Level Designer to take ownership while maintaining overall quality  
**Solution:** Set clear responsibilities, provided ongoing guidance, and reviewed work through milestone checkpoints  

### Key Achievements
- Delivered release-ready level on schedule  
- Maintained design quality and alignment with external partner expectations  
- Successfully mentored team members for independent decision-making  
- Implemented workflows that facilitated smooth collaboration between YAGER and Sharkmob
